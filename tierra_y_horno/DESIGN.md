## Brand & Style

This design system embodies warm minimalism, rooted in the visceral craft of wood-fired smoke, ancestral clay pottery, and slow culinary mastery from Oaxaca. It avoids touristic clichés, adopting an austere, editorial sophistication where texture, generous space, and deliberate typography mirror the hospitality and pacing of a destination dining institution.

The visual direction centers on Tactile Minimalism. Interfaces feel like unbleached cotton stock, sun-baked adobe, and cooling charcoal embers. Every digital touchpoint prioritizes high-impact editorial imagery of fire, raw local ingredients, and artisanal earthenware, framed by serene expanses of warm stone and hairline dividers. The atmosphere is quiet, confident, and deeply grounded.

## Colors

The palette is derived directly from the physical elements of Oaxaca's open hearths: charred wood, terracotta clay, wild mountain herbs, and limestone plaster.

- **Primary (`#1A1715`) - Oven Charcoal:** Dense, soot-tinted black used for high-contrast headlines, primary actions, and architectural framing lines. Never pure digital black.
- **Secondary (`#B8573E`) - Clay Brick & Ember:** A sun-baked terracotta red used deliberately for focal points, reservation badges, highlights, and primary active states.
- **Tertiary (`#636B46` / deep `#4E5633`) - Muted Agave & Sage:** An earthy, botanical olive green used for origin notes, sommelier selections, and subtle contextual affirmations.
- **Neutral & Canvas (`#FAF8F5`, `#F5F2EB`, `#EAE4D9`):** Layered warm white, adobe cream, and sun-dried river sand that serve as soft, organic ground planes rather than sterile gray backdrops.

Surfaces rely on tonal stepping: deep base cards sit on `#F5F2EB`, elevated popovers on `#FAF8F5`, and nested structural elements on `#EAE4D9`.

## Typography

The typographic pairing balances the literary heritage of Mexico with clean international culinary modernism.

- **Display & Headlines (`Newsreader`):** An optical editorial serif with warmth and character. Used for culinary titles, seasonal course names, quotes, and philosophical narrative statements. It carries the physical quality of ink pressed into natural fiber paper.
- **Body & Labels (`Manrope`):** A geometric, highly legible sans-serif with refined proportions. It delivers crisp readability for dish descriptions, ingredient sourcing details, pricing, and reservation flows without distracting from the editorial cadence of the headlines.
- **Typographic Discipline:** Label styles (`label-sm`, `label-lg`) frequently utilize uppercase tracking to denote origin regions, pairing notes, and table service categories.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow a fluid, asymmetric 12-column grid on desktop and 4 columns on mobile, punctuated by intentional pauses of empty canvas. Rather than packing content into rigid cards, components breathe with gallery-like margins.

- **Grid Geometry:** On desktop, editorial layouts allow full-bleed atmospheric photography alongside narrow 4-column typographic columns, evoking the layout of a bespoke hardcover cookbook.
- **Rhythm:** Spacing follows a relaxed scale. Content blocks are spaced with `space-xl` and `space-2xl` to establish an unhurried visual cadence.
- **Responsive Adaptations:** 
  - **Desktop (1024px+):** Generous 4rem margins; side-by-side juxtaposition of tasting menus and culinary provenance.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 2rem margins; menu pairings collapse into structured linear sequences.
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** 1.25rem margins; fluid full-width editorial ribbons with tight 1rem gutters for compact dish meta.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids digital-first elevations like high-spread drop shadows or synthetic blurs. Depth is tactile, physical, and quiet:

- **Tonal Stepping:** Surfaces establish elevation strictly via shifts in clay and stone tones. The base viewport sits on `#FAF8F5`, interactive panels and list rows sit on `#F5F2EB`, and recessed surfaces (e.g., date selectors, tasting menu drawers) rest on `#EAE4D9`.
- **Hairline Tactile Borders:** Panels and structural divides use a 1px solid border in `#1A1715` with 10% to 15% opacity (`rgba(26, 23, 21, 0.12)`). This evokes crisp paper edges and hand-cut book cloth.
- **Ambient Ash Shadow:** For elevated floating menus and booking modals, use a single, highly diffused, warm-tinted shadow: `0 16px 36px -12px rgba(26, 23, 21, 0.08)`. The elevation never feels synthetic; it suggests natural afternoon light falling across stone.

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary uses strict sharp corners (`roundedness: 0`). 

Rectilinear forms reflect hand-carved quarry stone, traditional masonry ovens, and high-end editorial book design. Buttons, media containers, cards, and modal sheets terminate in precise 90-degree edges. 

The single exception is circular framing for curated artisanal stamps (e.g., "Maíz Criollo", "Leña de Encino" seals) and table capacity avatars, which are strictly circular (50% radius) to contrast the architectural rectilinear grid.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#1A1715`, text `#FAF8F5`, 0px border radius, uppercase `label-lg`, padding `1rem 2rem`. Subtle transition on hover to `#B8573E` (Clay Brick).
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent background with a 1px border of `#1A1715` (30% opacity). Text in `#1A1715`. On hover, fills with `#EAE4D9` at 40% opacity.
- **Text Action:** Baseline underline positioned 4px below `label-lg` in terracotta (`#B8573E`), maintaining generous letter spacing.

### Menu Cards & Editorial List Items
- **Tasting Menu Row:** Borderless list item structured with asymmetric horizontal columns: Dish Title (`headline-sm`), provenance & cooking method (`body-sm` in muted `#636B46`), and price/pairing aligned flush right in `label-lg`.
- **Divider:** 1px hairline rule in `rgba(26, 23, 21, 0.12)` separating courses.
- **Photography Plate:** Editorial aspect ratios (4:5 vertical or 16:9 cinematic) with zero border radius. Images load with a warm, low-saturation tone, settling into rich firelit color on view.

### Chips & Badges
- **Ingredient & Pairing Tags:** Crisp rectangular badges with 0px radius. Background `#EAE4D9`, text `#4E5633` or `#1A1715`, typography `label-sm`. Minimal padding (`0.25rem 0.625rem`). No border.

### Inputs & Reservation Fields
- **Text & Date Fields:** Flat bottom-line inputs (1px border `#1A1715` at 25% opacity) resting on `#F5F2EB`. Labels sit as persistent small caps above the field (`label-sm`).
- **Focus State:** Bottom border deepens to `#B8573E` (Terracotta) at 2px weight without shifting adjacent content.
- **Guest Counter & Time Pickers:** Monospaced or clean tabular numerals (`Manrope`), framed in crisp 1px charcoal outline boxes.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Selection Controls:** Custom square indicators (0px radius) with hairline `#1A1715` borders. Active states fill with `#1A1715` containing an adobe-cream check or dot.

### Sommelier & Origin Footnotes
- Dedicated callout panels using a left vertical accent bar (2px solid `#636B46`), warm sand background (`#EAE4D9` at 50% opacity), and italicized serif body text (`Newsreader`) detailing mezcal terroir, heirloom corn varietals, and wood-firing duration.
